Respite 1.0.0-beta.1 is the first public release of the mod — a complete Vanilla+ sleep overhaul for Fabric 1.21.1. Everything below is new.

## Added

- **Time-lapse nights** — night accelerates through a time-lapse instead of skipping. The duration is configurable and kept in sync across the server.
- **AFK exemption** — players who are away from keyboard no longer block the night from advancing for everyone else.
- **Deep sleep** — how deeply you sleep scales with the lunar cycle; darker moons mean more restful nights.
- **Sleep-vote whisper** — a quiet chat message lets players know when others have gone to bed in multiplayer.
- **Restful saturation** — going to bed well-fed converts saturation into overnight healing.
- **Well-rested grace** — a brief morning boost rewards a full night's sleep.
- **Weariness** — skipping sleep builds a two-stage debuff. Reach the worst stage and exhaustion sets in.
- **Caffeinated Brew** — a campfire-steeped drink to take the edge off weariness when sleep isn't an option.
- **Bedroll** — a portable sleeping option for resting away from home.
- **Chronometer** — a time-of-day redstone block that outputs a signal proportional to the current hour, chimes an alarm, and reports moon phase through a comparator. A pocket-sized variant can be carried in your inventory.
- **Phantoms reworked** — phantoms now haunt high altitudes and appear only on the new moon.
- **Advancements** — a milestone tree tracks your journey through the new sleep system.
- **Configuration** — most of Respite's behaviour can be tuned in the server config.

## Fixed

- The Exhausted-stage blink suppression now activates only on hits that deal damage, not on every attack.
